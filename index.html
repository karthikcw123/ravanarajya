Getting around

Two cars, airport to airport — Day 3 is still a decision.

🚗Self-drive, 2 cars

6 adults + 2 kids + luggage doesn't fit one van comfortably. Two automatics gives space and flexibility on narrow hill roads.

  • Book fully-insured + zero-excess (Super CDW) — road conditions make scrapes likely
  • GPS-tracked, 24/7 breakdown line, replacement within 6–12h
  • 2 child seats; photograph every panel at pickup
  • Temporary licences for both drivers at the airport counter (LKR ~2,000/month)

⚠️The real risks

Not crime — roads and admin. Every stop on this route is mainstream tourist territory and safe.

  • No night driving on hill roads — buses overtake blind, elephants at dusk
  • Any accident → file a police report or insurance won't honour
  • Decline gem-shop "tips" and surf-board damage claims; pay deposits by credit card
  • Watch rip currents on the coast — swim where flagged

🚂🚗 Nuwara Eliya → Ella: no rental drivers, so it's us

We're not using rental-company drivers to relocate the cars anymore — that plan's out. Two live options instead, still to be decided as a family.

Option A · Split2 of us drive both cars Nuwara Eliya → Ella (~2h). Everyone else rides the blue train (~4h) and meets the drivers in Ella.
Option B · All driveAll 8 self-drive both cars Nuwara Eliya → Ella (~2h) together. No train, no split — simplest logistically.
Either wayPassports, cash and valuables stay with people, not locked in a car.

If Option A wins, book reserved train seats weeks ahead and agree who's driving early — hill roads are a lot on tired drivers.
